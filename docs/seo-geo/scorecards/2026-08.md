# SEO Scorecard — August 2026

_Source range: 28 days to 2026-08-23. Compared against July 2026._

**Headline — a click collapse, and the likely cause shipped mid-window.** Every tracked near-miss
keyword is still ranking well (several improved), but clicks vanished almost site-wide: total clicks
31 vs 32 in July, on impressions up to ~20,100 (+15%). CTR fell again to **0.15%** (was 0.18%, was
0.32% in June — the third straight monthly decline). Leads also dropped sharply. The prime suspect:
the **Storm design system relaunch** (theme + full header/nav rebuild, PR #648/#650, shipped
**30 July**) and the **services hub redesign** (PR #671/#672, shipped **8 August**) — both landed
inside this measurement window, right as clicks fell off. This is flagged as the leading hypothesis,
not proven causation; see the action item below to confirm or rule it out before next month.

> **Attribution note:** unlike June→July (on-page content maturing), this window's dominant story is
> engineering: a site-wide design system + navigation rebuild and a services hub redesign. No new
> targeted on-page SEO content shipped in-window. If the redesign is the cause, this is a
> template/rendering regression, not a content or ranking problem — different fix, different urgency.

## What moved & why

- **Clicks collapsed despite flat-to-improved rankings.** This is a new failure mode — June and July
  were both "position problems." August is a "position is fine, clicks vanished anyway" problem. The
  redesign (theme relaunch 30 July, services hub redesign 8 Aug) shipped squarely inside this window
  and is the leading hypothesis, but this is correlation flagged for investigation, not confirmed
  causation.
- **Leads dropped sharply, mostly phone calls.** `first_time_phone_call` 23 → 2 is a big enough drop
  to warrant a manual test of the click-to-call flow post-nav-rebuild, not just an SEO explanation.
- **Two July carry-over fixes both verifiably shipped** (checked live Sanity, not plan checkboxes,
  per [[scorecard-audit-live-sanity]]): `electrical-testing.seoDescription` and
  `owner.accreditations` are both populated. The testing cluster's continued slide isn't a missed fix.
- **Position gains kept coming where we'd already invested** — `electrician seddon` and `melbourne
electrical blog` both improved further into page 1, showing the on-page playbook still works when
  applied. The problem now is entirely downstream of ranking.

## Focus for next month (September 2026)

Priority order — **diagnose the click collapse before doing more content work:**

1. **Confirm whether the redesign broke or just delayed re-indexing.** Spot-check Google's cached
   snippet vs live page for 2-3 tracked URLs (`site:` search or Search Console URL Inspection tool) to
   see if Google is still serving a stale/broken snippet from before the theme change. If snippets look
   fine live but Search still shows old ones, this is a re-crawl lag that should self-resolve — re-check
   next month before making structural changes.
2. **Manually verify the click-to-call flow fires the GA4 event** post-nav-rebuild. The
   `first_time_phone_call` drop (23 → 2) is too sharp to attribute to organic softness alone without
   ruling out an instrumentation break in the new header markup.
3. **Hold off on new content until the collapse is understood.** Writing more posts won't help if the
   underlying issue is rendering/indexing — fix the suspected root cause first, this is a repeat of
   June's lesson (find the real problem before throwing content at it).
4. **If the redesign is cleared as the cause**, return to the standing priorities: convert the page-1
   near-misses into clicks (title/snippet tuning) and keep pushing `/services/commercial-electrician`
   (4,653 impr, pos 49, the largest buried-demand page on the site).

> The June→July arc proved on-page work moves rankings. August's job was supposed to be turning
> page-1 positions into clicks — instead a bigger question appeared: something (most likely the
> redesign) broke the position→click pipeline site-wide. September's first job is diagnosis, not more
> optimisation.

# SEO Scorecard — October 2026

_Compared against September 2026. Like-for-like basis: GSC `Queries.csv` (top 1,000 rows), the
same basis September's headline numbers used._

**Headline — the phone number fix did not unlock clicks.** October was the clean test, and it came
back flat. The old number was fixed in all 15 snippets on 16 September, so this window holds a
full month of the fix. On the top 1,000 queries, CTR is 0.22% (was 0.24%) and clicks fell from 39
to 29. The near-miss
keywords still earn zero clicks. Several of them also slipped: `commercial electrician altona
north` went from 1.63 to 4.76. The stale number was a real bug, but it was not the main cause of the
click collapse. The open question is now what Google shows above our listing (ads, map pack, AI
answer). That is #745, and it moves to priority one.

> **Attribution note:** no SEO work shipped in this window. Git has dependency bumps only. The one
> content-adjacent commit, the Google ratings update (#749), landed 24 September, before the window
> opened. Sanity shows no document changed after 16 September. An empty pipeline is a real finding:
> this month's movement comes from earlier work and from outside our control, not from new changes.

## What moved & why

- **No new work shipped, so nothing this month is attributable to new SEO changes.** Dependency
  updates only.
- **The phone number fix alone did not restore clicks.** CTR stayed at 0.22% over a full month with
  the fix live. That rules out the stale number as the whole explanation.
- **Rankings softened on the local terms** (`altona north` 1.63 → 4.76, `commercial electrician
altona` 5.96 → 8.50). With no changes on our side, this is most likely normal volatility or
  competitor movement. It is not evidence our content got worse.
- **Impressions fell for a second month** on both exports. September's truncation theory no longer
  holds.
- **Phone tracking now works.** 8 `phone_call_click` events from 6 users is the first usable phone
  baseline.

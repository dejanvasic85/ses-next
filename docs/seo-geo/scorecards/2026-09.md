# SEO Scorecard — September 2026

_Source range: 28 days to 2026-09-20 (GSC), 2026-08-24 → 2026-09-20 (GA4). Compared against
August 2026._

**Headline — the click collapse has a confirmed second cause, and it's fixed.** Rankings kept
improving sharply this month (several tracked near-misses now sit at position 2–6, one at
**1.63**), but clicks stayed at essentially zero on those exact pages. Issue #695's diagnosis
work found and fixed a real bug behind this: **15 published pages were showing Karl's old,
dead phone number in their Google search snippet**, fixed live in Sanity via #735 and verified
here — a zero-result GROQ query confirms no page still carries the stale number. A local-services
searcher reading a dead phone number straight off the search result has a concrete reason to
skip it, even at position 2. This doesn't yet explain the _whole_ collapse (see below), but it's
the first confirmed, fixed contributor rather than a hypothesis.

> **Attribution note:** the phone-number fix (Sanity content edit, no git diff, shipped via #735,
> closed 2026-09-16) lands inside this window, so some of this month's numbers already reflect it,
> but only for its last ~4 days. Its full effect is an October question. Separately, the
> `phone_call_click` GTM event (PR #732, live 13 September) replaces the broken
> `first_time_phone_call` counter — see the correction below and the diagnosis log for the full
> story. The August design-system/nav-rebuild hypothesis (PR #648/#650/#671/#672) for the
> **click** collapse specifically remains unconfirmed; #695 stays open pending October's read.

## What moved & why

- **CTR improved for the first time in three months (0.15% → 0.24%)**, the same month the stale
  phone-number snippets (#735) and the broken `tel:` links (#725) were both fixed. Directionally
  consistent with those being real contributors to the collapse, though the fixes landed late in
  the window so this is a partial read, not a confirmed cause-and-effect.
- **Rankings kept improving sharply** — `commercial electrician altona north` hit position 1.63,
  `mains power upgrade melbourne` jumped 13.4 places to 3.64 — but clicks on those exact terms
  are still zero. Position is not the constraint; something between position and click still is.
- **The August "lead collapse" was confirmed as a measurement break, not a real one.**
  `first_time_phone_call` was never defined in the codebase and stopped firing 30 July when the
  nav rebuild shipped, unrelated to actual call volume. Retired and replaced with
  `phone_call_click`, a number-independent event, live from 13 September (diagnosis log,
  issue #695, PRs #725/#732/#733).
- **The stale-phone-number fix is verified live**, not just checked off: a GROQ query against
  published Sanity documents for the old number returns zero results, per
  [[scorecard-audit-live-sanity]].
- **`/services/commercial-electrician` weakened further** (impressions and position both worse)
  ahead of its planned rewrite — useful as a clean pre-work baseline for measuring #735's second
  half once it ships.
